# Figma Style Guide — Main Hero (Glass Overlay Concept)

## 🎨 Color Palette

* Background Gradient: Soft Pink → Lavender
* Glass Panel Fill: White 30% opacity
* Shadow: Soft pastel purple glow
* Accent: Subtle warm peach

## 🖋 Typography

**Heading (EN)**

* Font: Playfair Display
* Weight: Regular / Semi-Bold
* Size: 64px
* Letter spacing: +1%
* Line height: 125%

**Subheading (KR)**

* Font: Pretendard / Weight: 300
* Size: 20px
* Opacity: 80%
* Line height: 150%

## 📦 Glass Card Style

* Width: Auto / Max 50%
* Padding: 48px top/bottom, 56px sides
* Border radius: 20px
* Fill: rgba(255,255,255,0.3)
* Blur: 20px
* Border: 1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.4)
* Shadow: soft pastel glow

## ✨ Motion & Micro Interaction

* Fade-in + slight blur to clear (duration 0.8s)
* Subtle float animation (Y: 2–4px) slow
* Hover on card: +4px lift, softer shadow

## 📏 Layout

* Card centered horizontally & vertically
* Outer margin: generous whitespace (airiness)
* Text stacking: EN → KR with 12px gap
* Divider element between 2 copies (thin line or dot)

## 💡 Visual Accents

* Slight sparkle textures or soft grain
* Tiny dots or soft glow as accent

## ✍️ Hero Text

**Where details become experiences.**
디테일이 경험이 됩니다.

**Kind and intuitive design.**
친절하고 직관적인 디자인.
